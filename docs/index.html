Reflectodoro

A Pomodoro app to force a short break and self-reflection at the end of every session - with hard to dismiss break screen.

Installation instructions

Windows may show a SmartScreen "unknown publisher" warning on first run — choose More info → Run anyway.

On macOS, the app isn't notarized yet, so Gatekeeper will block it the first time — right-click the app and choose Open, or run xattr -cr /Applications/Reflectodoro.app in Terminal.

On Linux, make the AppImage executable first (chmod +x Reflectodoro*.AppImage); some distros also need libfuse2 installed for it to run.

The Android build is a debug-signed APK (no Play Store listing or release keystore yet) — you'll need to allow "install from unknown sources" for your browser/file manager, and Play Protect may warn about an unrecognized developer.
For reliable break enforcement in the background, also grant Display over other apps and Notifications when onboarding asks, and disable battery optimization for Reflectodoro in Settings (on Xiaomi/Oppo/Vivo phones, also enable its "auto-start" toggle) — otherwise some OEMs can kill the background timer.

Note: If pre-builds are not working for your platform, you may do production build yourself using `Pre-requisite` and `Build a production installer` instructions given in Readme.

Wall-clock breaks ⏲

Work and break slots are pinned to fixed clock boundaries.

No free pass 🔒

The break screen can't be dismissed directly — you have to write what you did and wait for break time to be over.

Emergency exit ⚡

Type a random on-screen string to end the break early — still requires the reflection either way.

Wellness check 🌱

Wellness check at the end of break to track break items i.e. did short exercise, relaxed eyes, drank water.

Most Important tasks List 📝

Capture Most-important and Not to-do task list for day

Fully local and private 💻

All of your data is stored on your device. No cloud data transfer.

Auto-pause Media ⏸️

Auto-pause music/movies during breaktime, so you do not get spoilers 😜 when you get back.
